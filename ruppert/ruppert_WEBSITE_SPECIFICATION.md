# Ruppert Heizung · Sanitär · Solar — Website Specification

## Evidence-Led Brand Understanding

Ruppert is a local heating, sanitary and solar business in Osthofen, led publicly by Andreas Ruppert. The existing public site invites customers to discuss opportunities for energy savings in their homes through an appointment either at the company’s premises or on site. The public imprint describes Andreas Ruppert as a master central-heating and ventilation builder, state-certified HWK energy adviser, Solarteur and HWK business manager.[1]

The site’s job is to turn a household’s heating, sanitary, solar or energy-modernisation question into an appointment or a qualified request for an on-site assessment. It must remain factual: it can invite a consultation about possibilities for energy savings, but it must not promise savings, subsidy eligibility or a calculation without the current project facts.

## Visual Thesis

> **A precise local energy-modernisation site that makes Ruppert’s verified heating/sanitary/solar expertise and on-site assessment route the functional centre of the experience.**

| Token | Hex | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| `--solar-yellow` | `#C98700` | Functional solar/energy consultation cue; not a claim about performance. |
| `--heating-red` | `#9D3F33` | Controlled warmth marker for heating and hot-water contexts. |
| `--ink-blue` | `#244351` | Calm technical body and navigation colour. |
| `--field` | `#F3F4EE` | Restrained consulting-information background. |
| `--surface` | `#FFFFFF` | Clear fact and contact surface. |
| `--rule` | `#CFD5D0` | Functional boundaries. |

Use `Archivo` for headings and `Source Sans 3` for reading content. The unique content structure should be **“Energiefragen zuerst, Technik danach”**: heating/sanitary/solar scope, local assessment, then service sections. Do not reuse existing stock images or historical images without documented rights.
