# Schuhmacher Haus & Energietechnik GmbH — Source-Bound Website Specification

## Verified Identity and Content Basis

Schuhmacher Haus & Energietechnik GmbH is based at Ringstraße 23, 68753 Waghäusel-Wiesental. The official site lists telephone 07254 1461, fax 07254 60997 and info@schuhmacher-haustechnik.de.[1] [2]

The current website describes the firm as a medium-sized haustechnik company and states a company history beginning in 1947. It names Kurt Schuhmacher as managing director and owner.[1] [3] The official navigation and content identify Klempnerei, Wärme, Photovoltaik, Edelstahlkamine, Klima + Lüftung, Wasseraufbereitung, Regenwasser, Sanitär, Bad, Sanierung and Elektrotechnik. The site also publishes five guarantees for the bath area (complete service, appointment, cleanliness, hygiene and fixed price) and states a five-year guarantee on its own services.[1]
